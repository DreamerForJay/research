DFDS: Color-Aware Monocular Defocus Vehicle Distance Estimation with AI Segmentation and Physical Optics

Ongoing research · To be entered in the 2026 National University–Industry Innovation and Implementation Competition

A color-aware monocular vehicle distance estimation method combining Segment Anything, a back-focused single-image setup, CIECAM02 perceptual sharpness, and optimized color-channel weighting for interpretable estimation across different vehicle colors in CARLA.

Current results: RMSE ≤ 0.38 m for red, green, and blue vehicles; 13.44 FPS on an RTX 5080.
